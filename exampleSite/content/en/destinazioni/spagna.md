Spain was one of the longest and most varied stretches of our European leg: the white villages of Andalusia, the windmills of La Mancha, the greener and less touristy north. It's also the country where we ate best by far, between empanadas, tortilla and desserts we didn't expect.

Here you'll find itineraries region by region (Andalusia, La Mancha, the north), guides to towns worth a stop like Cordoba and Toledo, and our food discoveries along the way.
