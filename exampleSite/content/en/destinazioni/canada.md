Canada by campervan is the part of our trip where everything changed again: after Eurasia the van landed in North America and we had to adapt it to local standards, from the fresh/waste water fittings to third-party insurance for a vehicle that isn't registered there.

From there a different kind of journey began: the **Alaska Highway** heading north from Dawson Creek, the **Rocky Mountains** from Banff to Jasper along the Icefields Parkway, the national parks with their booking system, the **Okanagan Valley** among vineyards and lakes, Vancouver and Vancouver Island facing the Pacific.

Here you'll find all the practical guides, itineraries and stories written along the way: from what nobody tells you before shipping a campervan to North America, to tips on stopovers, fuel and water along Western Canada's scenic roads.
